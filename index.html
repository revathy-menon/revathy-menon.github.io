Machine Learning · Docking

DockScore 2.0

ML-augmented protein–protein docking scorer, with a two-stage cascade. The first XGBoost stage predicts CAPRI quality and filters out predicted incorrect decoys. The second GNN stage ranks the surviving decoys. Benchmarked on CAPRI Scoreset v2022 across 257 complexes and 100K+ decoys.

  • Python
  • XGBoost
  • GNNs
  • LightDock
  • CAPRI/DockQ
Approach

Recoded the original Perl-based DockScore in Python, computing six normalised interface descriptors (ISA, residue conservation via PSSMs, short contacts, hydrophobicity, spatial clustering, positive residues). Extended benchmarking to Docking Benchmark v5 with LightDock-generated ensembles; addressed class imbalance via SMOTE and down-sampling.

Highlight

Best classification achieved via knowledge distillation — a neural network trained to imitate XGBoost using Huber loss (λ = 0.25) — reaching F1 = 0.91, PR AUC = 0.99. Also explored graph neural networks with residue-level interface graphs and margin-ranking loss. Poster selected for EMBL AI and Biology Conference, Heidelberg (March 2026); ₹50,000 Infosys travel grant received.

EMBL Heidelberg Poster · 2026
Database · Bioinformatics

PASS2 v8 Database

Led the update of PASS2 — a database of structure-based sequence alignments covering 26,690 protein domains across 2,058 superfamilies. Achieved a 90.4% pipeline success rate and resolved 79 problematic superfamilies with k-means clustering.

  • Python
  • HMMER
  • MariaDB
  • PHP
  • BioPython
Approach

Drove the end-to-end automated pipeline for 1,244 multi-member superfamilies (SCOPe 2.08; <40% pairwise sequence identity). Integrated per-superfamily features: HMMs (HMMER), conserved residues, secondary structural motifs (Smotif), alignment statistics (AliStat), indel profiles (CUSP), intra-domain interaction networks (HORI), GO annotations, and putative interactors (STRING-DB).

Highlight

Implemented k-means clustering (elbow method; WCSS-based k selection) to recover 79 problematic superfamilies with >50% gap content or <40% secondary structure conservation — clusters aligned with known family-level annotations including CoA-dependent acyl transferases and Histone-fold superfamilies.

Published · Database Journal, 2025

Molecular Modelling · Collaboration

Actin–Tropomyosin Interface

Modelled the actin–tropomyosin interface across human and yeast isoforms using AlphaFold2-Multimer and iterative LightDock docking. Revealed a conserved electrostatic anchor mechanism and support for a "gestalt-binding" model for filament length dependence.

  • AlphaFold2
  • LightDock
  • FoldX
  • PPCheck
  • PyMol
Approach

Modelled hTPM1, hTPM4 and S. pombe CDC8 coiled-coil homodimers via AlphaFold2-Multimer; selected by PPCheck pseudo-energies + pLDDT. Performed iterative LightDock docking (200 swarms, 10 decoys/swarm, 1,000 GSO steps) per actin subunit. Built CDC8 temperature-sensitive mutants (A18T, R21H, E31K, E129K) in PyMol; energy-minimised with FoldX.

Highlight

Identified a conserved acidic hotspot (D254/hTPM1, D218/hTPM4, D131/pCDC8) forming salt bridges across all isoforms and species. Established an energetic hierarchy (pCDC8 > hTPM1 > hTPM4) supporting a "gestalt-binding" model: shorter filaments incur fewer geometric penalties. Collaboration with Dr. Mohan Balasubramanian, University of Warwick.

MD Simulations · Immunology

TRAM Dimer Interface Mutations

Investigated how dominant-negative mutations at the TRAM homodimer interface abrogate innate immune signalling. 200 ns MD simulations revealed elevated communication propensity, loss of conserved cliques, and prolonged allosteric paths in mutants.

  • GROMACS
  • HADDOCK
  • MDM-TASK
  • PSN-Ensemble
  • NAPS
Approach

WT/mutant dimers (P116H, C117H) built from the NMR structure (PDB: 2M1W); HADDOCK-modelled complexes (1,000 structures, 9 clusters); 200 ns MD × 3 replicates in GROMACS. Dynamic Cross-Correlation and Communication Propensity computed via MDM-TASK; Protein Structural Network analysis via PSN-Ensemble; allosteric path analysis via NAPS.

Highlight

Mutant dimers showed elevated CP, increased radius of gyration, loss of conserved cliques (WT: 1; mutants: 0), and prolonged allosteric shortest paths between functional sites — providing a structural basis for abrogated TRIF recruitment. Certified among the top 10% most-viewed papers published in Proteins (Jan–Dec 2024).

Published · Proteins, 2024

bioRxiv · 2026

Comparative Modelling of Actin–Tropomyosin Interfaces

Menon, R., Balasubramanian, M., Sowdhamini, R.

First

A stepwise computational docking pipeline combining AlphaFold2-Multimer coiled-coil models, experimentally informed residue-level restraints, and pseudo-energy analysis via PPCheck to build and evaluate actin–tropomyosin co-polymer models for three isoforms: hTPM1, hTPM4, and S. pombe Cdc8. Interface energetics reveal a hierarchy in which the shortest filament (SpCdc8) achieves the most stabilizing actin contacts, consistent with reduced geometric penalty along the actin helix. Hotspot mapping identifies conserved acidic residues across all three isoforms, and modelling of four temperature-sensitive Cdc8 mutations shows these substitutions destabilize the coiled-coil dimer without significantly affecting actin interactions.
